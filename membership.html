OPTIONAL · PAID

England Athletics registration

Paid membership covers England Athletics registration. It is separate from free club membership and is not required for our runs. You can register through Runhead. Registered athletes may receive:

  • £2 off England Athletics licensed road and multi-terrain races.
  • Eligibility for England road running competitions.
  • Discounts on some coaching and run leader courses.

Benefits depend on eligibility. See the current England Athletics benefits.

Complete the membership form and select the paid England Athletics registration option.
