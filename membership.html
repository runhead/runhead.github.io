England Athletics affiliation

You can also register with England Athletics through Runhead. Registered athletes may receive:

  • £2 off England Athletics licensed road and multi-terrain races.
  • Eligibility for England road running competitions.
  • Discounts on some coaching and run leader courses.

Benefits depend on eligibility. See the current England Athletics benefits.
